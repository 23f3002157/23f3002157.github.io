Current Research

Chaos-Inspired Ergodic Encoding for Combinatorial Optimization

Risshab Srinivas Ramesh, Dr. Prasant Misra, Dr. Nithin Nagaraj

Introduced Ergodic Solution Encoding (ESE): rather than learning instance-level decision policies, ESE encodes the population-level distribution of optimal solutions as the invariant measure of a Piecewise Linear Markov Map (PLM), fitted via the Inverse Frobenius–Perron Problem. Solution generation uses Orbit Walk and Ergodic Beam Search, exploiting deterministic chaotic dynamics to produce diverse, high-quality candidates without stochastic sampling or instance-specific training. On 15 TSPLIB benchmarks (N=10–500): matches MILP optimality at small scale; outperforms GA and learning-to-optimize baselines at medium–large scale at orders-of-magnitude lower inference latency. Manuscript prepared for IJCAI 2027.

Learning-Based Optimization of EV Routing with V2G Integration and On-the-Go Energy Harvesting

Risshab Srinivas Ramesh, Dr. Prasant Misra, Dr. Arjunan Pandaraswamy

Formulated the EVRP as a reinforcement learning problem, jointly optimizing routing, V2G energy sales, and on-route solar harvesting. Modelled time windows and energy dynamics as MDP constraints. On the Solomon benchmark dataset, the learned agent runs 18.85× faster than a genetic algorithm baseline; the GA achieves at most 21.23% higher solution accuracy, reflecting the practical speed-accuracy tradeoff at deployment scale.

When Explanations Fail: Measuring Attribution Drift in Corrupted Medical Images and an Architecture to Resist It

Risshab Srinivas Ramesh, Pradeep Kumar D, Roshani T S Udupa, Nishitha Mahesh

Introduced energy-in-mask (EiM), a chance-calibrated attribution metric that formalises a "sub-chance" test for saliency maps on segmentation models. Applied to CT lung nodules (LIDC-IDRI, n=150): the proposed ARX-SE-UNet, which conditions an SE-UNet backbone on a learned corruption embedding via FiLM-inspired blocks, is the only architecture whose EiM exceeds chance (0.0170 vs. c̄=0.0122) and achieves 6.2× the EiM recovery of the best baseline under CLAHE+BM3D preprocessing.

Leveraging Amazon Web Services IoT for Predictive Maintenance

Risshab Srinivas Ramesh

Reviewed predictive maintenance (PdM) as a condition-based strategy that combines real-time IoT sensor data with machine learning to anticipate equipment failures before they occur. Examined how IoT-enabled sensing supports anomaly detection and proactive maintenance scheduling, and discussed how integrating IoT data with maintenance management systems can reduce downtime, lower costs, and improve overall equipment effectiveness (OEE).
